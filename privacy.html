Pulse Spire Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026

1. App Name and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Pulse Spire, a mobile game for iPhone and iPad. The game does not require users to register an account or sign in to play.

2. Data We May Collect

The game may collect or use limited technical data needed to operate, protect, and improve the game experience.

  • Device identifiers such as IDFV for security, risk control, and anti-cheat purposes.
  • Basic app interaction data, such as gameplay progress, settings, and local game state stored on the device.
  • Crash, diagnostic, or performance information if it is provided by the operating system or integrated services.

3. Third-Party SDK Data

If remote advertising parameters are delivered and advertising is enabled, the game may initialize third-party advertising or security SDKs, including Yandex Mobile Ads, Chartboost, and DataEye/HSSecurity.

These SDK providers may collect data such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad impression data, ad click data, rewarded video view data, and product interaction data.

4. How Data Is Used

  • To provide rewarded ads and keep the game free to play.
  • To measure advertising performance and attribution.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, cheating, and invalid ad activity.
  • To maintain game stability, diagnose issues, and improve the user experience.

5. Data Sharing

We may share data with service providers that help operate advertising, attribution, security, and fraud prevention features. These providers may include Chartboost, Yandex, and DataEye/HSSecurity. We do not sell user account information because the game does not require an account.

6. App Tracking Transparency and Advertising

When required by iOS, the game will request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before using the IDFA for tracking across apps and websites.

If the user declines tracking permission, we will not use the IDFA for cross-app or cross-website tracking. Advertising partners may still use SKAdNetwork or similar privacy-preserving aggregated attribution methods where available.

Users can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Pulse Spire is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game or related services, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

8. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on the user's device unless the user deletes the app or resets progress. Data processed by advertising, attribution, or security SDK providers is retained according to those providers' own policies and legal obligations.
